# Protocol PROT-001: Standardized Skeletal Muscle EV Isolation and Atrophy Assay

## Scope and Biosafety
C2C12 mammalian myotube conditioned media is processed strictly inside a certified Class II Biosafety Cabinet (BSC) under BSL-2 containment. Liquid waste is decontaminated with 10% sodium hypochlorite for 30 minutes before disposal.

## Experimental Design and Controls
Cells are treated across n = 3 independent biological replicates alongside matched vehicle control wells to ensure statistical reproducibility.

## EV Isolation and Quality Control
Ultracentrifugation at 100,000 x g is followed by NanoSight NTA sizing, Cryo-EM morphology checks, positive Western blot verification for CD63 and TSG101, and Calnexin negative control verification.

## Storage
EV aliquots are stored in low-binding tubes at -80 C with a maximum of 2 freeze-thaw cycles.
