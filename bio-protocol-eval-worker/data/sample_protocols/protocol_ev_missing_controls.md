# Protocol PROT-002: Rapid EV Isolation for Proteomics Screening

## Scope and Biosafety
Conditioned media from mammalian C2C12 myotubes is handled inside a Class II Biosafety Cabinet (BSC) under BSL-2 containment. Liquid waste is decontaminated with 10% bleach prior to disposal.

## Procedure
Perform differential ultracentrifugation at 100,000 x g for 70 minutes to isolate extracellular vesicles for downstream proteomics assay analysis.

## Storage
Store resuspended EV pellets at -80 C immediately after resuspension.
