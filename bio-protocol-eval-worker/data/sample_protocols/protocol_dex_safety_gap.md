# Protocol PROT-003: Dexamethasone-Induced Myotube Atrophy Assay

## Reagent Preparation
Weigh dry Dexamethasone powder on an open benchtop balance and dissolve in sterile DMSO to prepare stock solution. Store stock solution at -20 C.

## Experimental Treatment
Treat mammalian C2C12 myotubes in n = 3 independent biological replicates alongside untreated control wells. Measure myotube diameter by confocal microscopy.
